# Image Credits

Question images in `assets/questions/` and signs cropped from them in `assets/signs/*.png` come from the
source database. Everything in `assets/diagrams/` was drawn for this book.

Signs 5.18, 5.28, 6.9, 8.2.4, 8.18 and 8.22 are reconstructions drawn for this book. Signs 5.24.1 and 5.26
were made by adding a red diagonal to the town plates from questions 1095 and 1096.
