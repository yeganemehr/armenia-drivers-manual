# Key Numbers

Every number the exam likes to ask, on one page. Use it for revision after you have read the lessons, because
each number has conditions that the lessons explain.

## Speed

| Who and where | Limit (km/h) |
|---|---|
| Any vehicle in a residential zone or courtyard | 20 |
| Any vehicle in a built-up area | 60 |
| Passenger car or truck up to 3.5 t, outside built-up areas | 90 |
| Passenger car or truck up to 3.5 t, on a motorway | 110 |
| Intercity bus, on all roads outside built-up areas | 90 |
| Other bus, or truck over 3.5 t, outside built-up areas | 70 |
| Passenger car with a trailer, outside built-up areas | 70 |
| Passenger car with a trailer, on a motorway | 90 |
| Towing a mechanical vehicle | 50 |
| People in a truck body, or organized groups of children | 60 |

## Stopping and parking

| Rule | Number |
|---|---|
| Stopping lasts up to | 5 minutes, or as long as boarding or loading takes |
| No stopping or parking before a pedestrian crossing, closer than | 5 m |
| No stopping or parking from the edge of an intersecting carriageway, closer than | 5 m |
| No stopping or parking from stopping point indicators, closer than | 15 m |
| No parking from a railway crossing, closer than | 50 m |
| No stopping if the gap to a solid line is less than | 3 m |
| No stopping at a dangerous turn with visibility under | 100 m |

## On the move

| Rule | Number |
|---|---|
| No overtaking before a railway crossing, within | 100 m |
| No U-turn with visibility under | 100 m |
| Dip your headlights from an oncoming vehicle, at not less than | 150 m |
| No motorway for vehicles that can't exceed | 40 km/h |
| At a railway crossing with no line or sign: stop from the barrier at | 5 m |
| At a railway crossing with no barrier: stop from the nearest rail at | 10 m |

## Signs

| Rule | Number |
|---|---|
| Warning signs in a built-up area stand before the danger at | 50 to 100 m |
| Warning signs outside built-up areas stand before the danger at | 150 to 300 m |
| "Emergency stop" sign in a built-up area, at not less than | 15 m |
| "Emergency stop" sign outside built-up areas, at not less than | 30 m |

## Towing and loads

| Rule | Number |
|---|---|
| Rigid coupling, not more than | 4 m |
| Flexible coupling | 4 to 6 m |
| Mark a load sticking out at the front or rear by more than | 1 m |
| Mark a load sticking out at the side by more than | 0.4 m |

## Your vehicle

| Minimum tyre tread | Number |
|---|---|
| Motorcycle | 0.8 mm |
| Truck | 1.0 mm |
| Passenger car | 1.6 mm |
| Bus | 2.0 mm |

## Licences and paperwork

| Rule | Number |
|---|---|
| National licence valid for | 10 years |
| International licence valid for up to | 3 years |
| Register a vehicle within | 15 days |
| Trailer allowed on your existing licence, up to | 750 kg |

## First aid

| Rule | Number |
|---|---|
| Tourniquet in warm weather, at most | 1 hour |
| Tourniquet in cold weather, at most | Half an hour |
| Cool a burn with water for | 20 minutes |
| Check an unconscious casualty's breathing for | 10 seconds |
